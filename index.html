Important legal and administrative note: This website retains only the 60-mark Academic & Research working matrix; the 20-mark Teaching Skill and 20-mark Interview sections are excluded. The 11.02.2026 Government Resolution prescribes cadre-specific assessment for regular teacher recruitment and does not provide a separate 60-mark schedule for Dean or Director KRC. Dean is a separate statutory officer post under the Dean Direction. The KRC Direction identifies Director KRC within the teacher definition and refers to Section 102, but does not itself set out a KRC-specific 60-mark matrix. Accordingly, the matrix on this site is a provisional working/reference matrix only and must not be represented as the formally approved selection scheme for either post unless the competent University authority confirms/adopts it. Eligibility, experience and statutory selection-committee requirements must be verified independently against the relevant Direction and advertisement. All marks must be supported by verified documents.
